The short version

This website does not track you. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party trackers. The only personal information collected is a first name and an email address, and only if you choose to subscribe to the newsletter.

What is collected

If you submit the newsletter form, the following is collected:

  • Your first name
  • Your email address

Nothing else is requested, and nothing is collected from visitors who do not submit the form.

How it is used

Your name and email are used to send you the Fulfillionaire newsletter. They are not sold, rented or traded, and they are not used for any unrelated purpose.

Who it is shared with

Newsletter subscriptions are processed and stored by our email service provider, who holds the data on our behalf and is not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Fonts and embedded content

Typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, your IP address is visible to Google. This is the only automatic request this site makes to a third party.

Links to YouTube, Apple Podcasts, LinkedIn, Instagram and other sites are ordinary links. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no browser storage.

Your choices

Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link, which takes effect immediately. You may also email info@fulfillionaire.com at any time to ask what information is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

Retention

Subscriber details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date above.

Contact
